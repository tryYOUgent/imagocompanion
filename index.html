For Couples

A private place to reflect before you dialogue.

Step-by-step prompts and examples make it easier to begin, easier to stay with the exercise, and easier to organize what you are learning.

The reflections work comfortably on phone, tablet, or computer. When a reflection is completed, clear organized results are emailed directly to the participant. My IMAGO Journey helps streamline the main themes across reflections so people can see patterns in themselves and their relationship.

Guided step-by-step reflection
Clear examples help participants understand what each prompt is asking.
Private by default
Their therapist does not see their answers. Their presenter does not see their answers. Their partner does not see their answers unless they choose to share them.
Organized results by email
Each completed reflection produces clear, structured results that participants can easily access.
Designed for the participant experience
The purpose is to help them learn more about themselves, recognize relationship patterns, and communicate more effectively.
For IMAGO Therapists

Help couples arrive ready to go deeper.

Between sessions, couples can complete guided reflections and bring their organized results back into the therapy room when they choose to share them. Where a specific tool provides it, they can also bring prepared dialogue material into session.

Less session time explaining the exercise. More session time deepening the work.

Before the workshop

Clear prompts and examples reduce the need to spend valuable session time over-explaining how to complete the reflection.

During the workshop

Organized results help you explore what the couple discovered, make connections, and deepen the IMAGO process.

Leaving the workshop

The platform supports the therapist; the therapist remains central to the clinical and relational process.

For Workshop Presenters

Less workbook friction. More room for the workshop experience.

IMAGO Companion helps participants arrive more prepared, stay with the reflection during the workshop, and leave with meaningful material already organized for further reflection.

Before the workshop

Support orientation and preparation so couples can come in more ready for the experience.

During the workshop

Participants complete guided reflections on phone, tablet, or computer, with examples that reduce confusion and help them stay with the exercise.

Leaving the workshop

Completed reflection results are emailed directly to participants, so they leave with organized material they can revisit after the workshop.

The structure is guided. The relationship work is still human.

IMAGO Companion does not replace the therapist, presenter, partner, or reflective process. It reduces logistical and cognitive friction around the exercise so more attention can remain on reflection and dialogue.

Reflections belong to the participant and are not automatically shared with a therapist, presenter, or partner unless they choose to share them.
