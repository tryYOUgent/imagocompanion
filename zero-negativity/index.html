Zero Negativity

Connection can be disrupted by words, tone, expressions, silence, or other interactions that feel hurtful or dismissive. This guided reflection will help you consider how you want to notice those moments, communicate them, and return to connection during the workshop.

There are no right or wrong answers. Reflect honestly and at your own pace.
