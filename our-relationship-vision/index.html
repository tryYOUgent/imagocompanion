How this works

From “My Vision” to “Our Vision”

Each partner created a picture of the relationship they want. Now you’ll look at those visions together.

First, you’ll notice the hopes and desires you share. These will become Our Relationship Vision.

Then, you’ll notice the things that matter to one of you but do not naturally overlap. These will become Our Relationship Potential.

“This isn’t about deciding whose vision is right. It’s about discovering what you share and becoming curious about where you’re different.”

Reflect side by side

Compare Your Visions

Read through both of your Relationship Visions together. As you go, notice what feels shared and what seems unique to one of you. Add what you notice to the appropriate space below. Your words don’t need to match exactly for something to be shared.

1

What We Share

What hopes, experiences, qualities, or desires appear in both of your visions?

For example: We both wrote about wanting more laughter and fun, feeling close and affectionate, making decisions as a team, and having a peaceful home.

Please add at least one thing you share before continuing.

2

What Is Different

What appears in one person’s vision but doesn’t clearly appear in the other’s?

Different doesn’t mean incompatible. You don’t need to resolve these differences here. For now, simply notice and capture them.

For example: One of us wrote about traveling and having more adventures together. The other wrote more about financial security and creating predictable routines at home.
