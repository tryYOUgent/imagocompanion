A conversation to share

Bring Your Visions Together

If your partner has completed their own Relationship Vision, set aside some time to read your visions together. You don’t need to agree on every statement. Instead, be curious about what each of you chose.

Notice where your visions naturally overlap. Notice where they are different. A difference does not necessarily mean one vision is right and the other is wrong—it may show you something important about what each of you longs for in your relationship.
