Imagine Your Relationship

A Relationship Vision begins by imagining the relationship you want to create together.

Over the next few questions, you’ll be invited to think about different parts of your relationship. You don’t need to find the “right” words. Write whatever comes naturally—hopes, wishes, things you miss, things you’d like to change, or things you already have and want to keep.

For now, simply focus on what matters to you. We’ll help you shape your thoughts into your Relationship Vision afterward.

You don’t have to have an answer for every question. If nothing comes to mind, simply continue. You can also go back at any time.
