How I Experience My Partner

Begin by naming the three things that matter most to you in each area.

What are THREE qualities in your partner that feel especially good to you?
These are qualities that help you feel drawn to, cared for by, supported by, or glad to be with your partner.
Need ideas?
Warm · Funny · Dependable · Affectionate · Thoughtful · Playful · Supportive · Calm · Generous · Curious · Loyal · Encouraging
What are THREE qualities in your partner that are especially difficult or painful for you?
This is about your experience, not about defining who your partner is.
Need ideas?
Distant · Critical · Controlling · Impatient · Unavailable · Defensive · Unpredictable · Dismissive · Withdrawn · Demanding
What are THREE things you most value receiving from your partner?
Think about what your partner gives or brings to the relationship that feels especially meaningful.
Need ideas?
Affection · Humor · Encouragement · Stability · Shared time · Practical support · Feeling wanted · Adventure · Companionship · Emotional presence · Reassurance · Partnership
What are THREE things you most long for from your partner or relationship?
Think about what you most wish you experienced more often from your partner or in the relationship.
Need ideas?
More affection · More emotional presence · Reassurance · Initiative · Appreciation · Curiosity about me · Calm during conflict · More shared time · Feeling understood · Feeling chosen · More partnership · More playfulness
Please complete all three responses in each part before continuing. You can always revise it later.
